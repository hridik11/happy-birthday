BIRTHDAY WISHING WEBSITE — HOW TO USE
=====================================

1. Unzip the folder.
2. Open  js/config.js  in any text editor (Notepad works) and change the names,
   bear messages, gift message, cheesy lines and the letter.
3. Open  js/memories.js  and write your memories.
4. Put your photos in the  images  folder (see images/README.txt for the exact names).
5. Double-click  index.html  to start the site. That's it!

THE 10 PAGES (each one links to the next)
  1  index.html     "Something special is on its way"   (night sky)
  2  page2.html     "Today feels a little different…"
  3  page3.html     photo 1
  4  page4.html     photo 2  + 3-2-1 countdown
  5  page5.html     HAPPY BIRTHDAY!  + photo 3 + confetti
  6  memories.html  your memories (edit js/memories.js)
  7  bear.html      the mocha bear: tap for messages, hug button
  8  gift.html      tap the gift box to open it
  9  heart.html     shoot the arrow, popup photo + cheesy line
 10  letter.html    tap the envelope to open your letter

THE ONLY THING THAT NEEDS INTERNET
  Three Google Fonts (Pacifico, Poppins, Caveat). Without internet the site still
  works with normal fonts. To use them offline, follow the steps at the top of
  css/style.css.

SHARING IT
  - Send the whole folder as a zip, or
  - Put it online for free with Netlify Drop (https://app.netlify.com/drop):
    drag the unzipped folder onto the page and you get a link to send.
