TIPS
  - Portrait or square photos look best (they are cropped to fit the frame).
  - Keep each photo under ~1 MB so the pages load fast (phone photos can be
    huge: shrink them with any free tool, e.g. https://squoosh.app).
  - The bear, gift box, arrow, heart and envelope are all drawn in code, so
    there is nothing else to download for them.
